# Supported applications

Only locations found on the current PC are shown.

## Adobe and texturing

Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Media Encoder, Camera Raw, Lightroom
Classic, Substance 3D Painter, Designer, Modeler, Sampler, and 3DCoat.

## 3D, CAD, rendering, and game tools

Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Houdini, ZBrush, Plasticity, Rhino,
Grasshopper, SketchUp, Nuke, Mari, Modo, Marmoset Toolbag, Marvelous Designer,
CLO, KeyShot, Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot.

## 2D and design

Affinity Photo, Designer, and Publisher; Krita; GIMP; Inkscape; CorelDRAW;
Corel Painter; Clip Studio Paint; paint.net; Aseprite; PureRef; and Capture One.

## Limits

- Unity editor preferences are read from the current-user registry.
- Environment-defined paths are detected for supported applications where known.
- Program Files, ProgramData, and Common Files targets may require administrator rights.
- Native plug-ins and binary preferences may not work across application versions.
- Project data, licensing, credentials, caches, logs, crash data, and autosaves are
  outside the intended migration scope.
- Always review target paths and **Preview** before restoring.
